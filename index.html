Introduction

This project analyzes scuba diving fatalities from data reports published by the Divers Alert Network (DAN). The primary objective of this study is to use data analysis, statistical methods applied to fatalities and variables, and network-based modeling of fatal pathways to evaluate what areas of prevention divers should focus on. The primary findings consist of which fatality pathways hold the most statistical significance, correlation findings between pre-existing health conditions and fatality disabling agents, and exploratory analysis for context.

Dataset
DAN annual fatality reports
Timeframe
2010 to 2020
Research Question
Which combinations of incident factors most strongly characterize fatal scuba diving pathways, and what statistical relationships drive prevention priorities?
