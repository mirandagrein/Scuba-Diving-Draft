Understanding Scuba Diving Fatalities

A comprehensive statistical analysis of diving incidents from 2010–2020, examining diver characteristics, environmental conditions, and incident factors associated with fatal outcomes.

[Hero image goes here]

Project Overview

This research analyzes scuba diving fatalities from data reports published by the Divers Alert Network (DAN). The primary objective is to apply statistical methods to understand the relationships and interaction effects among diver characteristics, environmental conditions, and incident-level factors associated with fatal outcomes.

Through exploratory data analysis and inferential techniques, this study evaluates associations between variables such as diver age, certification level, dive depth, and environmental conditions—providing evidence on distributional patterns and priority areas for future inquiry.
