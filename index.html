Scuba Diving Fatalities Project

Introduction (Abstract)

This project analyzes scuba diving fatalities from data reports published by the Divers Alert Network (DAN). The primary objective of this study is to apply statistical methods to better understand the relationships and interaction effects among diver characteristics, environmental conditions, and incident-level factors associated with diver fatalities. The analysis incorporates exploratory data analysis and inferential techniques to evaluate associations between variables such as diver age, certification or experience level, dive depth, and environmental conditions.
